**Images are now converted and compressed before they are uploaded.** A photo straight from an iPhone is 3–5 MB, enough for GitHub to warn about it; converted, the same photo goes up at around 150 KB. Every image can be checked and adjusted in the review window before anything is sent, and the image in your vault is never changed.

## Before you upgrade

- **Conversion is on by default.** Still images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, AVIF) are converted to JPEG and scaled down so their longest edge is at most 2000 px. GIFs and SVGs are always uploaded as they are, so animations keep moving.
- **Republishing an existing post can rename its images.** A PNG converted to JPEG is uploaded as `.jpg`, and the post's links point at the new file. The old `.png` copy is left in the repository, not deleted. To keep a post's images exactly as they were, set them to *Original* in the review window, or turn off *Convert images* in the settings.
- **Requires Obsidian 1.11.4 or later**, as before.

## New

### Image conversion
Each image in the review window has its own controls:
- **Format:** JPEG, WebP, or the original file, untouched. Changing it renames the upload to match.
- **Quality:** how hard the image is compressed.

Press **Convert** under an image to convert just that one, or **Convert all images** at the bottom of the window to work through them all, with a progress bar and a button to stop. A converted image shows a thumbnail of exactly what will be uploaded, its dimensions, and its size before and after. Change its format or quality afterwards and it is converted again on the spot. **Continue to preview** converts anything still waiting first, so nothing is uploaded unconverted by mistake, and the preview lists each converted image's size before and after.

The starting format comes from the settings, and is corrected once the image has been seen:
- An image with see-through areas switches to WebP, since JPEG would fill them with white.
- An image that converting would only make larger, or that the device cannot convert, is kept as it is.

The window says so whenever it changes something. A choice you make yourself is never overridden: if it cannot be converted, the window names the image and the reason, and waits for you to try again or choose *Original*.

### Gentle on phones
Images are converted strictly one at a time, and each photo's memory is released as soon as it is done, so a post full of photos does not get Obsidian closed on an iPhone. Opening the review window converts nothing and reads no image into memory; the thumbnails come straight from Obsidian.

### New settings
Under *Attachments*:
- **Convert images:** on by default.
- **Image format:** JPEG (the default, which every device can write) or WebP (smaller, and keeps transparency, but not every device can write it; where it can't, JPEG is used instead).
- **Image quality:** where each image's quality starts. Defaults to 80.
- **Maximum width or height:** defaults to 2000 px; 0 keeps the full size.
- **Leave small images alone:** an image already in the chosen format and no bigger than this (500 KB by default) starts out untouched, rather than being re-encoded for nothing.

## Fixed

- Date, date-and-time and number fields in the review window could shrink to a thin line on iPhone and iPad, especially when empty. They are now the same height as the text fields around them.

## Good to know

- Conversion happens on the device you publish from. The same image published from two different devices can come out slightly different, so it may be uploaded again as *changed*.
